This page lists the text books for the course and other text to support your reading. As an advanced course you are expected to use reference material to learn independently and support the learning activities.

All of these resources are freely available on the web or available free (to you) through the ANU library website.

## Text Books Used in the Course:

These books will be used frequently in the lectures and workshops. You should get to know them.

- Dannenberg, R. B. [Introduction to Computer Music (2nd Edition)](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~music/icm-online/icm-text-rbd-2021.pdf) (2021). _available free online_

- Puckette, M. [Theory and Technique of Electronic Music](http://msp.ucsd.edu/techniques.htm) (2007). _available free online_

## Pd Books and Resources:

These books help with the Pure Data computer music environment:

- Kreidler, J. [Programming Electronic Music in Pd](http://www.pd-tutorial.com) (2013). _available free online_

- Puckette, M. [Theory and Technique of Electronic Music](http://msp.ucsd.edu/techniques.htm) (2007). _available free online_

## SuperCollider and Live Coding Books and Resources

These books help with the SuperCollider computer music environment:

- Wilson S, Cottle D & Collins N. [The SuperCollider Book](https://library.anu.edu.au/record=b7381372). MIT Press. (2011). ([ANU Library link](https://library.anu.edu.au/record=b7381372))

- Collins, N. [SuperCollider Tutorial](http://composerprogrammer.com/teaching/supercollider/sctutorial/tutorial.html) _available free online_

## General Computer Music Books

These are general reference and research texts on Computer Music, we won't refer to these explicitly but they can give you further context and inspiration for your SMC journey.

- Cambridge Companion to Electronic Music ([ANU Library link](https://library.anu.edu.au/record=b4962499)).

- Müller, M.
  Fundamentals of Music Processing. Springer (2015) ([ANU Library link](https://library.anu.edu.au/record=b6458363))

- Dean, RT and McLean, A.
  [The Oxford Handbook of Algorithmic Music](https://www.oxfordhandbooks.com/view/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780190226992.001.0001/oxfordhb-9780190226992).
  Oxford University Press, (2018) ([ANU Library link](https://library.anu.edu.au/record=b5387425))

- Roads, C. The computer music tutorial. MIT Press (1996) ([ANU Library link](https://library.anu.edu.au/record=b1924457))

- Rowe, R. [Interactive Music Systems: Machine Listening and Composing](https://wp.nyu.edu/robert_rowe/text/interactive-music-systems-1993/chapter-1-interactive-music-systems/). The MIT Press (1993) (available online)
