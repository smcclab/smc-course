Course Title: COMP2710 Laptop Ensemble (6 units), Semester 1, 2020

### Learning Objectives

1. Describe & apply fundamental aspects of music computing including
   digital synthesis, algorithmic composition, and musical interface design.
2. Critically examine computer music systems and laptop ensemble performances.
3. Produce a computer musical instrument, composition or interactive
   media work and present it in concert.
4. Demonstrate collaboration skills with a laptop ensemble including
   being able to provide design feedback and critical self-reflection.

### Project Description

This project will involve producing a computer musical instrument/composition as
part of the ANU Laptop Ensemble and presenting it in concert at the end of the
semester. You will work on a performance of your own, but also engage with other
LENS students to give and receive feedback.

We will have weekly workshops during the semester where you will come (having
watched the weekly lecture content and submitted an audiovisual diary entry) to
engage with computer music theory and practice and do hands-on activities with
your fellow LENS members. You will develop & present a plan for your final
performance at the half-way mark of the course, and present your artefact in a
LENS concert at the end of the course.

### Assessment

- AV diary portfolio (30%)
- Project plan (20%)
- LENS performance (50%)

### Meeting Dates

- Weekly LENS workshop sessions
- Final concert at end of week 12
