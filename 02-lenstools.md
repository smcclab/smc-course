What tools do you need to take part in LENS? Let's take a look.

## Laptop?

LENS is a laptop ensemble, so if you have a laptop and want to use that,
then that's great. There _are_ other possibilities, for instance, you
can use Android or iOS devices (e.g., Charles' [_Ensemble
Metatone_](https://metatone.net) used iPads), or even a Raspberry Pi?

During classes we expect you to bring your device (and charger), and some headphones.

We have a limited number of [headphones](https://www.audio-technica.com/cms/headphones/2d52765b7d84656c/index.html), [audio interfaces](https://www.behringer.com/Categories/Behringer/Computer-Audio/Interfaces/UM2/)), and [MIDI controllers](https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/novation-impulse-49) for students use in class or borrow if you need to. Get in touch if you have a good use case for these!

## Computer Music Programming

Learning the basics of computer music programming is part of this
course. We've listed some tools below that are good options for
getting starting with computer music and should be accessible to
everyone.

- [Pure Data (Pd)](https://puredata.info) - a visual computer music
  environment (hot tip--> also runs on Android/iOS phones and tablets
  and on Raspberry Pi!)
- [Supercollider](https://supercollider.github.io) - a text-based
  computer music environment (used to be taught at ANU School of Art!)
- [Extempore](https://extemporelang.github.io) - another text-based
  computer music environment (partly developed at ANU!)
- [Max/MSP and Ableton
  Live](https://www.ableton.com/en/live/max-for-live/) - Max is a
  visual computer music environment (related to Pd!), and is bundled
  with Ableton Live, so if you happen to have a Live license ($$$)
  then you'll have Max. Due to the $$$ we don't recommend this _that_
  much, but it's a great system if you want to invest in it.
  

## Code examples in Pure Data

Charles has written a series of introductory examples to Pure Data: [ComputerMusicIntro examples](https://github.com/cpmpercussion/ComputerMusicIntro)

You can also find lots of great Pd examples in the [rjlib library](https://github.com/rjdj/rjlib/)
