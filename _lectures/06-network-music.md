## Exercise: Designing Network Music-Making Systems

1. Divide into groups
2. Pick  _one music making scenario_  (e.g., amateur choir, pub rock covers band)
3. Come up with  _two different network music making ideas_  for this scenario from different time/space situations.

(Don’t worry about technical or implementation limitations.)
