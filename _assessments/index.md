## Outline

Half of your assessment for this course is based on your analysis & reflection
on the key computer music concepts presented in the course, as demonstrated
through your [portfolio]({% link _assessments/01-audiovisual-diary.md %}) 
and your [LENS demo]({% link _assessments/02-demo-day.md %}). The other
half is based on your [final performance & artefact]({% link
_assessments/03-lens-performance.md %}) at the end of semester.
