### Description

In the week 8 workshop session you will give an in-class presentation describing
your plan for your final LENS performance, and also submit a document describing
your LENS performance and how it will address the assessment criteria.

If you're not sure exactly what to cover in your presentation, here are a few
questions to help you get started (note: this is not a checklist---just some
stimulus material to sharpen your thinking).

- are there any artists/pieces which you've used for inspiration? how will your
  performance/artefact explore & extend the things in that work? (it's ok to
  show short clips of these artists in your presentation)

- is there a key idea or concept that's at the heart of your
  performance/artefact?

- what's _unique_ about your performance/artefact---what is it that makes
  yours stand out from the crowd?

- how are you going to make it a compelling overall _performance_ (including the
  visual aspect)?

- you need at least one of your classmates to be involved as an ensemble
  member---in what ways can they be involved? what's your pitch to them?

### Specification

Your submission must include:

- a **500-word document** (pdf format) describing your proposed LENS performance
  and how it will address the assessment criteria

- your **visual aids** (pdf format) and any necessary patch
  files/code/sounds/video assets from your presentation

You must present your performance plan to the rest of the class in a 6 minute
in-class presentation (followed by 3 minutes of questions). You should have
visual aids (e.g. slides, video) and you may include a live demo if you wish
(you must submit these ahead of time---see [below](#submission-process)).

These presentations will happen over zoom (because #covid19), so if there are
any technical aspects of your presentation (e.g. screen-sharing, videos, live
demos with sound) make sure you test them out first (if you want to do a test
run of the technology, let us know on slack and we can arrange it).

### Submission process {#submission-process}

You must submit your document, slides and associated files through GitLab. All
students will present their work to the rest of the LENS cohort during the week
8 [workshop timeslot]({% link _workshops/index.md %}) the following day.

### Marking criteria {#marking}

Your proposal will be assessed entirely based on the provided (pdf) document &
visual aids. The marking criteria (based on the [course learning outcomes]({%
link 02-outline.md %})) are:

- technical quality & ambition of your artefact/performance with regards to the
  fundamental aspects of music computing covered in this course

- understanding and engagement with the design and performance challenges around
  computer music interfaces

- scope for collaborative ensemble interaction in your performance

- clarity of communication (words, visual aids, demos) in your in-class
  presentation
