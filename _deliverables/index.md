Half of your assessment for this course is based on your analysis & reflection
of the key computer music concepts presented in the course, as demonstrated
through your portfolio (30%) and your in-class presentation (20%). The other
half is based on your final artefact/performance at the end of semester.

| Deliverable                                                                  | Due Date        | Weighting       |
